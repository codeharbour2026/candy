The Candy Bowl

Rules & safety

Before launch: The school must approve student-to-student food sales, account rules, allergen handling and handover arrangements.

Be fair. Be safe. Be sweet.

  • Be honest about what you are selling and show accurate product and package information.
  • Do not create fake listings or accept orders you cannot fulfil.
  • Pay the agreed amount when collecting an order. Cash payments happen directly between buyer and seller.
  • Arrange handovers only at school-approved locations and times.
  • Follow school rules about food sales, allergens, ingredients, packaging and hygiene.
  • Do not list prohibited, unsafe or unapproved products.
  • Do not harass, threaten, deceive or pressure another student.
  • Do not manipulate reports or make knowingly false or malicious reports.
  • Do not publish private information such as school email addresses, phone numbers or home addresses.
  • Report problems honestly. Reports are reviewed; a report alone does not automatically issue a strike.

Strikes and suspensions

Moderation should be based on documented rule violations and a fair review. Users should be told the reason and be able to appeal. The intended policy is three active strikes → a 30-day suspension from new marketplace activity. The active-strike policy must be clearly defined by the school and administrators before launch.

Payments

The Candy Bowl records orders only. It does not process card payments, bank transfers or other online payments. Buyer and seller arrange cash payment directly at an approved handover.
